Three steps. Right there in the aisle.

BookNod works in your phone's browser. Add it to your home screen and it opens like any other app.

1

Set your rules

Make a profile for each child and decide what's okay: language, violence, romance, scary themes, bullying and more. Your 6-year-old and your 10-year-old can have different rules.

2

Scan or type the book

Point your camera at the barcode on the back, snap the cover, or type the title. Works in the library, the bookstore, or the school book fair.

3

Get a clear answer

Read, Skip, or Check, with the reasons behind it, for each child. No digging through reviews.

Questions parents ask

How does BookNod decide?

BookNod looks at what's known about the book (its content, themes and intended age) and compares that with the rules you set for each child. It shows you the reasons, so you can see exactly why it said Read, Skip or Check.

Is it always right?

No tool is. BookNod is a fast first opinion, not a replacement for your judgement. "Check" means it's a close call and worth a closer look.

Do I need to download anything or make an account?

No. BookNod runs in your phone's browser. You can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app. There's nothing to sign up for.

What if it can't find a book?

It tells you, and it doesn't use up one of your checks. Try snapping the cover or typing the title instead.

Can the whole family use one Family Pass?

Yes. One pass covers up to 5 phones. Enter your pass code on each one.

Can I see how it works under the hood?

Yes. BookNod's code is public on GitHub under the Functional Source License.
